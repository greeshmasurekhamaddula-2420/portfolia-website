My Contribution

How I participate, support teams, and take responsibility.

I believe contribution is not only about building something alone, but also about supporting the team, taking initiative, and communicating clearly. Here are some ways I try to contribute.

Hackathons

Biodegradable Plastic Bags using Starch

Role: Speaker & researcher · Team project

Worked with a friend’s team on an idea to develop biodegradable plastic bags using starch to reduce environmental impact. I contributed by researching and helping to present the idea.

TalentHunt – Find Teammates for Projects

Role: Team leader · Hackathon concept

Led a team to design a platform that helps students discover teammates for projects and hackathons based on interests and skills. I coordinated the work and guided the overall idea.

AYUSH Herb Traceability (Smart India Hackathon)

Role: Speaker · SIH participation

Participated in SIH with an idea to track AYUSH herbs from farmers to users using blockchain. I focused on explaining the solution clearly and representing the team during presentations.

Teacher Productivity AI App (24-hour Hackathon)

Role: Speaker & researcher · 24-hour hackathon

Worked on an idea to build an AI-based app to reduce teachers’ workload by automating repetitive tasks. I helped in researching features and communicating the solution.

These ideas are in different stages, and I am working towards turning more of them into working projects.

Roles & Responsibilities

Teaching & Mentoring

I take online classes for my juniors to teach C programming for their semester. I try to keep the class interactive, use simple explanations, and relate concepts to real-life examples so that they feel confident with the subject.
